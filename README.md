## Customer Onboarding: Diet Information

When welcoming new customers, collect the following details to tailor dietary guidance and product recommendations:

1. **Goals and preferences**
   - What are your primary health or fitness goals?
   - Do you follow any specific dietary patterns (e.g., vegetarian, vegan, keto, gluten-free)?
2. **Allergies and intolerances**
   - Do you have any food allergies or intolerances we should know about?
   - Are there ingredients you prefer to avoid?
3. **Typical intake and habits**
   - What does a typical day of meals and snacks look like for you?
   - How many meals do you prefer per day, and do you track calories or macros?
4. **Lifestyle factors**
   - How active are you, and what type of exercise do you do?
   - Do you have schedule constraints that affect meal timing or preparation?
5. **Hydration and supplements**
   - How much water do you drink daily?
   - Do you take any supplements or medications that may impact your diet?

Use these prompts during onboarding to build a clear picture of each customer’s needs before creating a nutrition plan or recommending products.
